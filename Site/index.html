        About AnaloGêmeo

        AnaloGêmeo searches Earth for places that resemble exploration targets on the Moon and Mars. Each Earth site gets a similarity score per dimension (terrain, materials, thermal, water and ice, dust, habitability, operations). The final score is a weighted average, so you can change the mission profile and watch the ranking change. Every site also shows where the analogy works and where it breaks.

        Status: the scores shown now are sample values. They will be replaced by the output of our data notebooks (NASA Earthdata, AppEEARS, Copernicus) in data/locais.json.
